## Feats

- [ ] [Superior Summoning](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/feats/general-feats/superior-summoning)<br>
      Note: 1d3 when more than one monster<br>
      Benefit: When a summoning spell conjures more than one creature, summon 1 additional creature. Prerequisites: Augment Summoning, CL 3.

- [ ] [Evolved Summoned Monster](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/feats/general-feats/evolved-summoned-monster)<br>
      Note: 1-point evolutions [Eidolon Evolutions](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/classes/base-classes/summoner/eidolons)<br>
      Benefit: Summoned creatures gain one 1-point eidolon evolution. Prerequisites: Augment Summoning, Spell Focus (conjuration), Knowledge (planes) 5 ranks.

- [ ] [Inscribe Magical Tattoo](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/feats/item-creation-feats/inscribe-magical-tattoo-item-creation)<br>
      Benefit: Create magical tattoos. Prerequisites: caster level 5, Craft Wondrous Item, Spellcraft 5 ranks; creation cost is half the market price.

- [ ] [Tattoo Attunement](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/feats/general-feats/tattoo-attunement)<br>
      Benefit: Attune to one additional magical tattoo; changing the attuned tattoo takes 24 hours. Prerequisite: character level 5 and one magical tattoo.
